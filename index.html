          What file types can I send?
          
STL files only, up to 14 MB. If you don't have a model yet, describe your idea in the order form and add a rough size.

        

        

          Which material should I choose?
          
Right now everything is printed in PLA, which suits display pieces, toys, and indoor use. More materials may be added later.

        

        

          Custom or ready to ship?
          
Ready-to-ship pieces are already printed, so you only choose a color. Go custom when you need a different size, shape, or material.
